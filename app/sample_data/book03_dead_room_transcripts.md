## Chapter 1: The Forty-Two Hertz Notch

The anechoic chamber at Site 11 had been dead to ambient noise for forty years, but when Finch patched the Bruel & Kjaer preamplifier into Channel A, the VU meter slammed against its right-hand red stop with a violent magnetic click.

"Check the ground loop," Finch said, pressing the padded earphones hard against his temples. "We are picking up fifty-cycle mains hum from the transformer yard."

Kovacs shook his head without looking up from the spectrograph monitor. His glasses caught the emerald phosphor glow of the raster line. "It isn't sixty cycles, Harlan. And it isn't fifty. It's forty-two point four zero Hertz, phase-locked, with harmonic sidebands at eighty-four point eight and one hundred twenty-seven point two."

Finch stepped onto the isolation springs. The floor swayed imperceptibly beneath his rubber soles.

"The sensor array is fifty yards ahead," Kovacs whispered. Even at two feet distance, his voice sounded flat, dry, and starved of resonance, swallowed instantly by the mineral wool baffles.
